# Natural disasters and climate

## Natural disasters and climate

### Climate change

Climate change is affecting Uganda. Extreme and unusual weather events are becoming more frequent and may affect your travel plans. Monitor local media for the latest information.

### Seismic activity

Uganda is located in an active seismic zone.

Even minor earthquakes can cause significant damage.

### Rainy seasons

The rainy seasons usually extend from March to May and from September to November. This can vary from year to year due to climate change. Seasonal flooding can hamper overland travel and reduce the provision of essential services, particularly in rural areas. Roads may become impassable due to mudslides and landslides. Infrastructure may be damaged.

* Monitor local media for the latest information, including on road conditions
* Stay away from flooded areas
* Monitor weather reports
* Follow the instructions of local authorities, including evacuation orders
