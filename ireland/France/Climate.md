## Climate

Temperatures in France have had a tendency to increase significantly in recent years. For extended periods from May to August, temperatures across the country can reach the high 30s, often exceeding 40 degrees Celsius.

Travellers are advised to keep up to date with alerts on the website of the national weather service, [**Méteo France**](https://vigilance.meteofrance.fr/fr).

During high temperature periods, travellers are advised to be mindful of the health risks. Take normal precautions such as drinking water regularly and avoiding physical exertion. It is also advisable to keep indoor spaces as cool as possible, for example by keeping shutters closed.

Travellers should consult the [**HSE website**](https://healthservice.hse.ie/staff/procedures-guidelines/staying-safe-in-hot-weather/) for more information on staying safe in hot weather.

### **Wildfires**

Wildfires routinely affect southern and central areas of France in periods of hot weather. The French weather service provides [**a fire alert map**](https://meteofrance.com/meteo-des-forets) showing wildfire risk. Citizens should monitor the news and local authorities social media and websites regarding outbreaks of fire.

Citizens in, or near, areas affected by wildfires should exercise extreme caution, monitor news updates and follow the instructions of the local authorities (e.g. local ‘mairie’, commune, or prefecture).

Irish citizens intending to travel to affected areas should contact their accommodation or travel provider for the latest updates ahead of travelling.
